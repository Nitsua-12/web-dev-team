Fine Line Tattoos

Fine line tattoos use thin, precise needle work to create delicate linework, small lettering, minimalist illustrations, and detailed botanical or geometric designs. There's little to no shading — the line itself carries the piece.

Because the lines are so thin, placement and skin type matter more than with bolder styles — healing and long-term hold are worth talking through before booking.
