Custom Tattoos

A custom tattoo starts with your idea, not a pre-drawn design off a wall. The artist works with you on placement, size, and composition before any linework happens, so the final piece is built for your body and your reference, not resized to fit it.

Custom work usually takes a consultation first — bring reference images, a rough idea, or just a concept, and the design gets refined from there.
