Realism Tattoos

Realism tattoos aim to reproduce a photograph or lifelike image on skin — portraits, animals, objects — using fine shading and blending to create depth and texture rather than bold outlines.

It's one of the more technically demanding styles, since skin doesn't hold detail the way paper does; larger pieces and simpler reference images generally translate better than small, highly detailed photos.
