Traditional Tattoos

Traditional (or "American traditional") tattoos use bold black outlines, a limited and highly saturated color palette, and classic imagery — anchors, roses, swallows, daggers — built for longevity as much as looks.

The style's thick lines and solid color fills are specifically what make it hold up well over decades, which is part of why it's stayed a staple rather than a trend.
